About Matendo Day Care Centre

A caring place where young children learn, play and grow with confidence.

Who We Are

Building a Strong Foundation for Every Child

Matendo Day Care Centre provides an early learning environment where children can develop important skills through play, creativity and guided learning.

We believe every child deserves care, encouragement and opportunities to explore the world around them.

Our aim is to support children's early development and prepare them for their next educational journey.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, caring and engaging learning environment that supports children's early development, creativity and confidence.

Our Vision

To nurture confident, curious and responsible young learners who are prepared for future learning and positive participation in society.
